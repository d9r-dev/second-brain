## Definition
The norm of a vector, denoted as $|\overrightarrow{\text{v}}|$ or $||\overrightarrow{\text{v}}||$, represents the vector's magnitude (length).
* For a vector $\vec{v} \in \mathbb{R}^n$, its norm is calculated as 
			$\|\vec{v}\| = \sqrt{v_1^2 + v_2^2 + \dots + v_n^2}$
Norms provide a way to measure the size or length of vectors in vector spaces.
$||\overrightarrow{v}||$ in Euclidean Space is also called $L_2$ norm.
