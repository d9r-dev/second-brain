Evergreen post about [# Things I Learnt The Hard Way (in 30 Years of Software Development)](https://blog.juliobiason.me/thoughts/things-i-learnt-the-hard-way/) from Julio Biason. It covers advice for programming, working in teams, persona growth and so on. Give it a read and you might take away something

--- 

If you want to achieve that your team is more self managing you should not stop giving your co-workers always advice. That makes them passive and reliant on them. Instead start to coach them, which means helping them to overcome their obstacles. At least, that is what Lisa Gill claims in[ this post.](https://reimaginaire.substack.com/p/want-teams-to-be-self-managing-be) 

I am often also an "automatic advice addict" myself, and not only at work. But I give my best to become more of a coach and listener.
