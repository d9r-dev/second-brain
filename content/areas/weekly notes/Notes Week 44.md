After Twitter updated their blocking policy again there was some kind of exodus from Twitter to BlueSky. For example [Kelsey Hightower](https://bsky.app/profile/kelseyhightower.com) suspended his Twitter account and moved to BlueSky. There are also some other great people posting there like [Thorsten Ball](https://bsky.app/profile/thorstenball.com). Maybe we finally see a bigger move from tech twitter to BlueSky?

---

The results for the [State of CSS 2024](https://2024.stateofcss.com/en-US) are in. Winner over all seems to be the :has() pseudo-class. Other than that the results are pretty much unspectacular. Everybody uses Tailwind, most people use SASS/SCSS and so on. 

---

Also, the results for the [State of Frontend Survey 2024](https://tsh.io/state-of-frontend/) are in. Some results: 
- Typescript is the de facto standard for frontend development
- Vite is the de facto standard build tool nowadays
- React is still the most used frontend framework
