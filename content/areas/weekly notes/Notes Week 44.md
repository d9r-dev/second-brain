After Twitter updated its blocking policy, there was a notable of exodus from Twitter to BlueSky. For example [Kelsey Hightower](https://bsky.app/profile/kelseyhightower.com) suspended his Twitter account and moved to BlueSky. Several other tech personalities, like [Thorsten Ball](https://bsky.app/profile/thorstenball.com), are now actively posting there. Could this signal a larger shift from Twitter to BlueSky?

---

The [State of CSS 2024](https://2024.stateofcss.com/en-US) results have been released. The clear winner appears to be the :has() pseudo-class. Beyond this highlight, the results were largely predictable: Tailwind continues to dominate as the preferred CSS framework, while SASS/SCSS remains the most popular CSS preprocessor.

---

The [State of Frontend Survey 2024](https://tsh.io/state-of-frontend/) have also been published. Some results: 
- TypeScript has become the de facto standard for frontend development
- Vite has emerged as the predominant build tool
- React maintains its position as the most widely used frontend framework
---

Thorsten Ball had a great [conversation with David Albert](https://registerspill.thorstenball.com/p/in-conversation-david-albert), the co-founder of [the Recurse Center](https://www.recurse.com). Their discussion covered various topics, including the nature of learning, whether learning should be enjoyable, and the decision-making process behind building custom tools.
