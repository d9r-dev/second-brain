# Vegan East German Potato Salad

## Ingredients:

- 1.5 bags of potatoes
- Apples, diced
- Pickles, diced
- Smoked tofu, diced
- Pickle juice
- Pepper
- Salt
- Vinegar
- 600-800g of soy yogurt

## Preparation:

1. Boil the potatoes with their skin on.
2. Let the potatoes cool and then peel them.
3. Cut the potatoes into small pieces.
4. Add the finely diced apples, pickles, and smoked tofu.
5. Pour in some pickle juice.
6. Season with pepper and salt, and add a splash of vinegar.
7. Mix everything well.
8. Add soy yogurt on top and stir it in.
9. Taste and let it rest.
10. Add more salt or pepper to taste if needed.

## Note:

For 1.5 bags of potatoes, use about 600-800g of soy yogurt. Start with less yogurt as you can always add more if the salad appears too dry.
